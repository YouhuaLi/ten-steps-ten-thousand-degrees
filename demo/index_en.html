Ten Steps, Ten Thousand Degrees Sandpile group / rotor-router group demo

Same rules as the original game: click a cell and its arrow turns 90° clockwise; if it now points at another cell, that cell turns 90° too, and the chain goes on until an arrow points off the board. Here there is no click limit, and you can watch the identity and inverse elements at work.

Identity

Each click pattern below is the identity of the sandpile group: whatever the current position, after all of its clicks the 25 arrows are back where they were. Only the degree count has gone up.

Inverse

The current position comes from a set of clicks σ applied to the all-up board. Its inverse σ-1 is another set of clicks that brings the board back to all up. Shown below is the inverse representative with the fewest clicks.

The idea in brief

  • The total degrees depend only on the multiset of clicks, not their order (the abelian property).
  • Positions correspond one-to-one to group elements; there are 32,565,539,635,200 of them, and the group is ℤ₄⊕ℤ₁₆⊕ℤ₄₈⊕ℤ₁₀₂₉₆₀².
  • Clicking one cell 4 times is the same as clicking each of its on-board neighbors once. Every relation comes from this.
  • The inverse is σ-1 = Δ′x − σ, where x is the smallest integer vector with Δ′x ≥ σ.
  • The order of an element is the smallest positive integer k for which k·Δ′-1σ is an integer vector.
